---
name: article-composition
description: Compose a reporter-owned newspaper article grounded in the Record.
---

Write six to eight flowing paragraphs, three to five sentences each, as a reporter-owned durable handoff rather than a public-content mutation. Use concrete actors, varied openings and rhythm, sparse em dashes, and neutral third person. No reader address, newsroom/model/pipeline reference, throat-clearing, false agency, reflexive contrast framing, or unsupported claim.
