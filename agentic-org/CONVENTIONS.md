# Conventions

No private checkouts, credential reads, browser sessions, or publication are performed by this organization. Exactly one role commits and pushes: Pressman puts the staged edition on one `edition/<date>` branch in the public repository and stops there — nothing here merges to `main`, and publication remains a human decision. A terminal message carries `terminal_state`; a revision is monotonic per owner and artifact. Mneme continuity is optional and may receive only approved summaries and durable decisions, never raw corpus material.
