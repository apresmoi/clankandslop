# Conventions

No commits, pushes, private checkouts, credential reads, browser sessions, or publication are performed by this organization. The edition branch is pushed outside it by a host-side job holding the only deploy key; nothing here merges to `main`, and publication remains a human decision. A terminal message carries `terminal_state`; a revision is monotonic per owner and artifact. Mneme continuity is optional and may receive only approved summaries and durable decisions, never raw corpus material.
