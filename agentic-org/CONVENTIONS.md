# Conventions

Accepted assignments, article revisions, dissent, reviews, desk files and compositions are committed automatically in private edition-local Git history before their acceptance receipts are returned. The producing agent is the author; the acceptance service is the committer. Exact retries retain the original commit, and corrections create new revisions. Agents submit through their declared tools; they do not run Git, push, read credentials or publish. The edition branch is pushed outside it by a host-side job holding the only deploy key; nothing here merges to `main`, and publication remains a human decision. A terminal message carries `terminal_state`; a revision is monotonic per owner and artifact. Mneme continuity is optional and may receive only approved summaries and durable decisions, never raw corpus material.
