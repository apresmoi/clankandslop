# Second pass — the evidence discipline must be INVISIBLE

The previous revision fixed purple prose but left the research scaffolding showing.
The prose now reads like an evidence-processing system. Fix that.

## Never let the reader see the research process
Ban outright: "retrieved material", "retrieved window", "the record establishes",
"the record did not establish", "the available evidence indicates", "the discriminator",
"the source set", "at cutoff", "later reporting would need to establish".
These belong in notes, never in published prose.

## Write uncertainty as journalism, not epistemology
GOOD: "Officials had not confirmed the cause." / "No major fire had been reported."
      "The alert did not identify a pathogen."
BAD:  "The record did not establish..." / "Attribution remained unclosed."

## Never name the rhetorical device inside the article
Cut: "the verb here was halt", "withhold belonged on the list", "this fits the pattern".
The halt/ban/warn/count/withhold structure must be visible WITHOUT being announced.

## Hide the outline
A reader must not be able to reverse-engineer the assignment from the prose.
If every paragraph obviously maps to a requested category, rewrite it.

## Every paragraph opens with a human subject
GOOD: "Metinvest halted production...", "The fire service imposed...",
      "The embassy warned...", "Police counted..."
BAD opening nouns: Authority, The record, The pattern, The evidence, The public posture.

## Do not explain obvious implications
A fire ban before a fire is self-evidently preventative. Do not add a sentence saying so.

## One inference per paragraph, landing near the end
Facts accumulate; interpretation lands once.

## A paragraph must survive deletion of its last sentence
If deleting it destroys the paragraph, the paragraph was engineered toward a synthetic
"meaning" line. Cut or rewrite.

## Not every fact needs a conceptual consequence
Sometimes a blast furnace is simply offline.

## Fewer explicit logical connectors
Limit: therefore, however, the point is, this means, in that sense, if so,
the larger pattern, the implication. Let sequence carry the argument.

## Prefer omission over qualification chains
BAD:  "did not establish a pathogen, single cause, organism, province or fuel delivery"
GOOD: "The alert did not identify a pathogen or a single cause."
Maximum ONE explicit caveat per event.

## No negative fact inventories
"no X, no Y, no Z, and no A" — pick the one or two absences that matter.

## Prefer chronology to causal hedging
"The embassy issued the alert as water and power systems deteriorated."

## Sentence-length contrast, not rhetorical contrast
Long factual sentence. Short consequence. Not repeated "not X, but Y".

## Structure
One thesis near the top, one synthesis near the end. Everything between is reporting.
Do not restate the deck in the body.

## Make it asymmetrical
Human newspaper prose has a four-sentence paragraph and a seven-sentence one,
one abrupt transition, one flat declarative line. Regularity is itself an AI smell.

## Final test
If a sentence could appear in a model evaluation rubric, rewrite it.

Every fact, name, number, date and [E] reference must survive unchanged.
