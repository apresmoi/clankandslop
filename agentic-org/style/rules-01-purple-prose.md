# Revision guidelines — apply all of these

1. Prefer concrete sentences over abstract framing. Name actor, action, object, consequence. Avoid "the pressure field", "the public posture", "the material chain", "the administrable act".
2. Cut "not X, but Y" aggressively. One or two per piece maximum. Rewrite directly.
3. One metaphor per paragraph, maximum. Do not stack oracle/clerk/weather-map/storm/hand.
4. Do not make every paragraph prove the thesis. Report the event first; let the pattern emerge.
5. Put the factual payload early — first or second sentence: what happened, where, who said it, what changed.
6. One principal thought per sentence. Over ~30-35 words, split it.
7. Ordinary verbs: "stopped production", "issued a ban", "reported 1,344 deaths" — not "reached a public posture".
8. Stop narrating epistemic hygiene. Cut "the careful version", "the responsible verb", "the discriminator is".
9. State uncertainty plainly: "Officials had not confirmed who was responsible."
10. Not every sentence an aphorism. One memorable line per section.
11. Reduce thesis repetition. Opening + one synthesis + ending is enough.
12. Transitions informational, not literary.
13. Do not force symmetry across examples. Different stories, different lengths.
14. Specific nouns over conceptual nouns: "blast furnaces", "fire restrictions", "missing-person records".
15. Remove sentences that merely redescribe the previous sentence.
16. Rhythm: fact -> qualification -> implication. Do not mix all three into every sentence.
17. Let simple facts remain simple.
18. Use conceptual vocabulary sparingly.
19. Read aloud for cadence. Avoid: long setup -> semicolon -> contrast -> polished final clause.
20. Optimize for first-pass comprehension.
21. Preserve the strong device (halt/ban/warn/count/withhold); simplify the prose around it.
22. "Could Reuters print this sentence?" test on the factual layer.
23. 80-90% clear reporting, 10-20% stylized prose. This piece is currently the reverse.
24. Ban repeated AI templates: "not X but Y", "less X than Y", "the point is not X", "what matters is", "the deeper thing is", "in that sense", "this is where", "the real X is", cadence triplets.
25. Final edit: subtract 20-30%. Cut atmosphere, reiteration, cleverness.

Every fact, number, name, date and source in the original must survive unchanged. Do not invent anything. Do not add facts. This is a revision, not a rewrite of the reporting.
